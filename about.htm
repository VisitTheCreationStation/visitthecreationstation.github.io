What is the Creation Station?

Welcome! The Creation Station is a writing resource in the form of a website, made by a Girl Scout Ambassador for her Gold Award project to encourage creativity in the local and global youth. This website is a compilation of resources to print, use, and share with your children and the kids in your community! Creativity is plummeting in the recent years, and we hope to reintroduce real creativity to today's children.

Where can I share the Creation Station?

The Creation Station can be shared anywhere and with anyone! The goal of the project is for it to grow and reach new people, and you can help! Learn how to share the Creation Station in YOUR community HERE and help the project grow!
