What is the Creation Station?

It's a project made by Girl Scout Ambassador Alivia Peterson to encourage creativity in the local youth. It is a compilation of free resources to print, use, and share with your children and the kids in your community! Creativity is plummeting in the recent years, and I hope to reintroduce real creativity to today's children.

Where can we share the Creation Station?

The Creation Station can be shared anywhere! The goal of the project is for it to grow and reach new people, and you can help! Learn how to share the Creation Station in YOUR community HERE and help the project grow!

Why use the Creation Station?

Use the station to learn how to write books and illustrate them! Fuel your creativity with the abundance of resources available.
