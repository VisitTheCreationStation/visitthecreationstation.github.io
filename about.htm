What is the Creation Station?

The Creation Station started as a Girl Scouts Gold Award project, Completed by Ambassador Girl Scout Alivia Peterson in 2025. As a young girl, she had the vision to make a space for children to write and illustrate their own books to share with other local children. She took her Gold Award project as an opportunity to make that dream come true!

Where is the Creation Station?

The Creation Station can be anywhere! The goal of the project is for it to grow, and you can help! Learn how to make your own Creation Station HERE and help our project grow!
Above is a map of the current Creation Stations. Every time a new one is added, the map is updated to include the new Station.
